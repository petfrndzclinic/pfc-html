Our Services

Comprehensive Pet Healthcare

From routine checkups to emergency care, we provide a full range of veterinary services to keep your pets healthy and happy.

Vaccination
Comprehensive vaccination programs tailored to your pet's age, lifestyle, and health needs. Protect against rabies, distemper, parvovirus, and other preventable diseases with our expertly administered vaccines.
Deworming
Regular deworming treatments to eliminate intestinal parasites and maintain your pet's digestive health. We use safe, effective medications tailored to your pet's specific needs and weight.
Routine Health Checkup
Comprehensive physical examinations including temperature, heart rate, respiratory assessment, dental checks, and early disease detection to ensure your pet stays healthy year-round.
Disease Treatment
Expert medical care for acute and chronic conditions including infections, allergies, digestive issues, and more. We provide accurate diagnosis and effective treatment plans.
Nutrition Advice
Personalized dietary consultations to optimize your pet's nutrition based on age, breed, activity level, and health conditions. Get expert guidance on food selection and feeding schedules.
Microchipping
Quick, painless microchip implantation providing permanent identification for your pet. Increases chances of reunion if lost and meets travel requirements for international pet relocation.
Pet License
Obtaining a license helps authorities monitor the pet population for public health reasons, provides a way to easily identify lost pets, and is often required for activities like dog shows.
Diagnostics
State-of-the-art diagnostic services including digital X-rays, ultrasound, blood work, urinalysis, and fecal testing for accurate health assessments and treatment planning.
